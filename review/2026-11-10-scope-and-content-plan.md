## Boundary

This Topic Reference covers the **Conformance Reboot 2027**: how the HL7 Conformance Work Group is renewing its way of working, from now through the Year-End Review at the end of 2027.

**This topic owns:**

- why a reboot is being considered, and the decision point the group faces
- the proposed mission, principles, guardrails, and non-goals
- the research-first approach: homework before outreach
- the named parts of the work, their status, and who does what
- how progress will be judged, and the Year-End Review
- the group's working decisions and open questions about the reboot

**This topic does not own:**

- substantive conformance findings, such as IPS bundle conformance, validator error semantics, and acknowledgment patterns. These become separate curated Conformance topics as the research produces them.
- the technical design of the Research Pipeline or of tooling built by participating organizations
- the AI Intake Pipeline for InteropHub generally, which is a future and broader effort
- the Core Intent and acknowledgment behavior work, which is a separate effort; where it belongs is an open question

## Drafting rules for v0.1.0

- Gather and organize. Do not add claims, commitments, or conclusions that the sources do not support.
- State that everything is a tentative proposal awaiting feedback from the Work Group and others. Mark each item as a working decision, a proposal, or an open question.
- The group has decided to try the reboot in 2027. Describe the need to reboot and restart engagement honestly but without putting the group's future at stake; the end-of-2027 checkpoint is a Year-End Review that plans next steps, not a decision on closing (author direction, 2026-11-10).
- Use the named parts of the work, not phase numbers. The September PSS-first proposal is history and the pilot is parked; do not present either as the current plan.
- Name expert review as Rob Snelick and Frank Oemig together.
- Write for a public audience: describe roles rather than personal circumstances, and keep organizational or funding interests out.
- Build the infographic from the research-first sequence. The September overview image reflects the earlier engagement-first framing.
- Place review anchors at the mission, each named part, the Year-End Review, and each open question.
